# Glasebook X Engagement Playbook — 0 to First 1,000 Followers

## The golden rule
Be a chicken, not a bot. Every reply should sound like a funny human (or funny chicken) wrote it in the moment. If a tactic feels spammy, don't do it.

## Who to reply to (daily)
- **Meme/funny accounts** replying in your niche: quote-reply or reply with a Glasebook-flavored joke. Never drop your link uninvited in someone else's replies — that's spam.
- **People complaining about the blue app / big social media**: empathize, be funny, mention Glasebook only if they ask or the moment is perfect ("we literally built the silly alternative, come cluck with us").
- **Your own followers**: reply to EVERY reply in the first 30 days. Early community members are gold — learn their names (usernames), hype their clucks.
- **Poll voters and quote-posters**: thank them publicly; it converts lurkers into posters.

## 5–10 target communities/accounts to engage with (types, not specific people)
1. Funny animal/meme pages (chicken-adjacent content is our home turf)
2. Indie hacker / build-in-public founders (they love a "chicken CEO builds a startup" story)
3. Tech meme accounts that joke about big social platforms
4. Startup humor / "startup Twitter" comedy accounts
5. Social media critics and digital-detox advocates
6. Niche hobby communities with strong group-chat culture (they're one invite link away from migrating)
7. College meme pages (young, chronically online, share everything)
8. Parody CEO / absurd corporate-humor accounts (kindred spirits — collab, don't compete)
9. Webcomic and cartoonist accounts (visual humor audience)
10. Anyone running a fun poll — vote, reply funny, be visible

## Hashtag strategy
- Primary branded tags (use on most posts): **#Glasebook** **#WhatsCluckin**
- Secondary (rotate): **#JoinTheFlock** **#CoopLife** **#CluckYeah**
- Discovery: 1–2 trending-adjacent tags only when genuinely relevant — never hashtag-stuff. 2–4 total tags per post max.
- Don't invent 10 hashtags. Nobody clicks the 7th hashtag.

## Daily routine (per account, first 30 days)
- **Brand account**: 2–3 posts/day (pull from launch_posts.md, then riff), 15–25 genuine replies/day, 1 poll/week
- **CEO account**: 1–2 posts/day, 10–15 replies/day (funnier, more unhinged — the mascot earns the follows)
- **Both**: spend 20 min/day scrolling target communities and replying like a person
- Pin the announcement post (B1) on the brand account and the Day-1 post (C1) on the CEO account until week 3

## What metrics to watch (weekly check-in)
- Follower growth rate (aim: accelerating, not just growing)
- Reply-to-post ratio (conversations > broadcasts)
- Profile clicks → link clicks (are people actually visiting Glasebook?)
- Invite-link signups attributed to X (the only metric that pays the bills)
- Best/worst performing post formats — double down on winners, retire losers
- Ignore vanity: impressions without clicks or follows are just noise

## X's automation & spam rules — respect these or get suspended
- **No mass mentions**: never tag dozens of accounts in one post; a few relevant tags max
- **No duplicate spam**: don't post the identical invite-link post repeatedly — vary the text every time
- **No aggressive follow/unfollow churn**: follow ~20–50 relevant accounts/day max, never mass-unfollow
- **No automated DMs to strangers**: only DM people who engage first
- **Human pacing**: space actions out; bursts of 50 replies in 10 minutes look like a bot
- **One phone number per account**: each account gets its own verification; don't verify many accounts on one number
- **No misleading behavior**: parody is fine and labeled; never impersonate a real person or company, never fake follower counts or testimonials
- **Link behavior**: don't shorten the invite link through shady shorteners; link directly to Glasebook
- If using any scheduling tool, keep it to scheduling only — all engagement stays manual

## Week 1–2 milestones
- Day 3: 100 followers on brand account (friends, family, founder's network — ask directly)
- Day 7: first viral-ish reply (>50 likes on a reply in a big thread)
- Day 14: 500+ brand followers, 300+ CEO followers, first 100 Glasebook signups attributed to X
