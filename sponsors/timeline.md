# Glasebook Sponsor Timeline — Milestone-Gated Plan

Owner: Aric Komlance · Written 2026-09-08

**The honest headline: outreach before ~10k X followers is premature.** A sponsor pitch from an account with 300 followers isn't a pitch, it's spam — it burns the contact and the brand. Until the gates below open, 100% of effort goes to growth (see `MONETIZATION.md` Phase 0). This timeline exists so we move the *instant* a gate opens, not before.

All follower counts and revenue figures are **estimates for planning, not promises**.

---

## 🥚 0 followers — today (2026-09-08)

**Do:**
- Follow every target on the target list from both @Glasebook and @CEOofGlasebook.
- Begin the soft campaign: @CEOofGlasebook replies funny (never salesy) to target brands' posts 2–3x/week. Priority: Duolingo, Liquid Death, Steak-umm, MoonPie, Youtooz, Among Us.
- Save this package. Revisit at each gate.

**Do NOT:**
- No DMs. No emails. No pitches. No "collab?" replies. Zero outreach.
- Don't even hint that we want sponsorships. We're just a funny chicken in their replies.

**Gate to next:** 1,000 X followers.

---

## 🐣 1,000 followers — the proof-of-life gate (est. weeks 4–8)

**What it means:** Strangers are following a chicken. The concept has legs.

**Do:**
- Keep the reply-guy campaign running — by now some social media managers know the chicken by name.
- Start a private "warm contacts" note: which brands liked/replied/followed back. These become the first pitches at 10k.
- If the Glasebook app has 1,000+ registered users with 30% weekly activity (the Phase 1 gate in MONETIZATION.md), begin Stripe setup and Premium prep — monetization starts with *users*, not sponsors.
- Optional: one *inbound-only* test — pin a cluck on the @Glasebook profile saying "launch partner spots opening soon 🐔" and see who bites. Never chase; let them come.

**Do NOT:**
- Still no outbound pitches. 1k followers is a milestone, not leverage.

**Gate to next:** 10,000 X followers.

---

## 🐔 10,000 followers — outreach opens (est. months 3–6)

**What it means:** We have a real audience and a real voice. Now it's a business conversation.

**Do:**
- Send first pitches to the 10k-gate targets: Liquid Death, Takis, Slim Jim, MoonPie, Steak-umm, G Fuel, Opera GX, Among Us, Youtooz, Displate, Exploding Kittens, Tipsy Elves, Streamlabs.
- Start with the warmest contacts first (brands that already engaged with us).
- Lead with the smallest ask: one $25 promoted cluck or one roast-or-toast post. Land 2–3 small wins, screenshot the results, use them as proof for bigger pitches.
- Publish results publicly ("brand X's cluck got 3x our average pecks") — transparency is marketing.
- Attach the one-pager (`pitch_one_pager.md`) to every email.

**Targets for this phase (estimates):** 3–5 paying sponsors, $200–1,000/mo total sponsor revenue.

**Do NOT:**
- Don't pitch the 25k/50k-gate brands yet. Patience is leverage.

**Gate to next:** 25,000 X followers.

---

## 🚀 25,000 followers — the mid-tier unlock (est. months 6–12)

**What it means:** We're a known quantity in meme-brand circles. Mid-size brands can justify the experiment.

**Do:**
- Pitch the 25k-gate targets: Pringles, Skittles, Pop-Tarts, Prime, Celsius, Subway Surfers, Wingstop, Raising Cane's, Dave's Hot Chicken, Manscaped, Cameo, CapCut, Canva.
- Introduce the branded-challenge format ($1,000+ est.) — it needs a big enough audience to work.
- Start the "Founding Flock" scarcity narrative: partner spots are limited, early pricing locks forever.

**Targets for this phase (estimates):** 8–12 active sponsors, $2,000–8,000/mo total sponsor revenue.

**Gate to next:** 50,000 X followers.

---

## 🏆 50,000 followers — the big leagues (est. year 2+)

**What it means:** We're a genuine cultural artifact. Big brands' social teams have heard of the chicken.

**Do:**
- Pitch the 50k-gate targets: Hot Cheetos, Oreo, Red Bull, Monster, Clash of Clans, Roblox (partnership), Popeyes, Discord (partnership), Duolingo (the dream collab).
- KFC at 100k+ or on viral momentum — the moonshot.
- Roll out Phase 2 ad products per MONETIZATION.md: self-serve CPM dashboard ($8–12 CPM est.), coop takeovers, API access.
- Hire (or delegate to the agent) a part-time partnerships person — at this volume, manual outreach doesn't scale.

**Targets for this phase (estimates):** 15–25 active sponsors + self-serve ad revenue, $10,000–30,000/mo total.

---

## Standing rules (all phases)

1. **Gates are gates.** If growth stalls, the timeline waits. Never pitch from weakness.
2. **Warm beats cold.** A brand that laughed at our replies for 3 months converts 10x better than a cold email.
3. **Small asks first.** Every relationship starts with the tiniest possible yes.
4. **Real numbers only.** Every metric shared with a sponsor is real, dated, and verifiable. One inflated media kit ends the program.
5. **The March 2027 check-in stands.** Per MONETIZATION.md: if Glasebook hasn't found real traction by March 2027, the honest move is a post-mortem, not more sponsor decks.
