## What is Glasebook?

Glasebook is a brand-new social network with one thesis: **nobody shares boring.** Users post "clucks," "peck" the ones they like, and gather in "coops." It's a parody-inspired, fully original platform — legally distinct from Meta, with a clear non-affiliation stance — and it's run publicly by its mascot CEO, Clark Cluckerberg (a chicken). The joke is the product, and the product is working: every mechanic in the app (invite codes, referral leaderboards, shareable profiles) is built for viral growth.

## Why sponsors should care

- **The most unserious ad inventory on the internet.** Our audience doesn't tolerate boring ads — which means the brands brave enough to be funny here get remembered. Attention is the only currency that matters, and parody is an attention machine.
- **Native by design.** Promoted clucks look like clucks, not banner ads. They're naturally ad-block resistant and feel like content, because they are.
- **Founder-led distribution.** The chicken CEO posts daily, replies to everything, and does the kind of mascot marketing agencies charge six figures to imitate badly.
- **Trust positioning.** We publicly commit to never selling user data. Sponsors get a brand-safe-ish, scandal-free environment — the anti-Facebook pitch.
