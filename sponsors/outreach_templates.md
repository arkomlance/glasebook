# Glasebook Sponsor Outreach Templates

Voice guide: funny, confident, self-aware. We're a chicken running a social network — lean into the absurdity, never apologize for it. Short beats long. One joke per message, max two. Always end with a clear, low-pressure ask.

**Rules:**
- Never send before the follower milestone gate for that target (see `target_list.md`). No exceptions — a pitch from a 200-follower account is spam.
- Personalize the bracketed parts. Never send a template verbatim without filling them in.
- If they say no, reply "no worries, the coop door stays open 🐔" and move on. Never double-pitch the same brand within 90 days.

---

## Template 1 — X DM (first contact)

For use only after 2+ weeks of funny (non-salesy) replies to their posts.

>

## What never to do

- Don't mass-DM. Every outreach is manual, personalized, and spaced days apart.
- Don't pitch in public replies — replies are for jokes; pitches go to DMs/email.
- Don't inflate numbers. If they ask for metrics, share real ones with dates. "We're small and growing fast" beats a fudged media kit every time.
- Don't chase. One follow-up, then silence. Desperation is the only brand-unsafe thing about us.
