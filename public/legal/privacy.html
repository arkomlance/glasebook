Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Glasebook ("we", "us", "our"), owned by Aric Komlance, respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: the name, email address, and password (stored as a one-way hash) you provide when you register.
  • Profile content: your bio, avatar, and cover images.
  • Your posts ("clucks"), comments, and eggs (likes), including any text and images you upload.
  • Direct messages: the content of your 1:1 conversations.
  • Connections: your friend relationships and friend requests.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as log entries, device type, and pages visited, used for operating and securing the Service.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service (e.g., showing your feed, delivering messages).
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent abuse, spam, and fraud.
  • To communicate with you about the Service, such as notifications you have enabled.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

3. Cookies and sessions

We use cookies and server-side sessions to keep you signed in and to protect against abuse (for example, security tokens that prevent cross-site request forgery). If you disable cookies, you may not be able to use the Service.

4. Sharing of information

Content you choose to make public (such as public posts and public profile information) is visible to other users and may be indexed by search engines. We may share information when required by law, to enforce our Terms of Service, or to protect the safety of our users. We do not share your private messages or non-public content with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete content or close your account, we will delete or de-identify your information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law or for legitimate security purposes.

6. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Access the information associated with your account via your profile and settings.
  • Correct or update your profile information.
  • Delete your posts, messages, and other content, or close your account entirely.

To exercise any of these rights, or to request a copy of your data, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

7. Children's privacy

Glasebook is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy here with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact
