How to file a takedown notice

Your notice must include the following information (per 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)):

  1. Your physical or electronic signature (or that of a person authorized to act on your behalf).
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed (or a representative list of works if a single notice covers multiple works).
  3. Identification of the material you claim is infringing, and information reasonably sufficient to let us locate it on Glasebook (e.g., a link to the post, profile, or image).
  4. Your name, address, telephone number, and email address so we can contact you.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the copyright owner's behalf.

Upon receiving a valid notice, we will remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing material and notify the user who posted it.

Counter-notice

If you believe your content was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the same designated agent. Your counter-notice must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the material that was removed or disabled, and the location where it appeared before removal.
  3. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, address, and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court in your district (or, if outside the United States, any judicial district in which Glasebook may be found) and that you will accept service of process from the person who filed the original notice.

If we receive a valid counter-notice, we may restore the material between 10 and 14 business days after receipt, unless the original complainant files a court action seeking to restrain the user from engaging in the allegedly infringing activity.

Repeat-infringer policy

We will, in appropriate circumstances, disable or terminate the accounts of users who are repeat infringers of copyright. We also reserve the right to remove content and suspend accounts for infringement without prior notice.

Abuse of the process

Please note that under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), any person who knowingly makes a material misrepresentation that content is infringing may be liable for damages. Do not make false claims.
